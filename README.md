# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence (BI) dashboard built using Power BI to analyze retail e-commerce sales, regional performance, product categories, and customer segments.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project delivers actionable business insights through an intuitive and responsive Power BI report. It helps stakeholders track overall revenue, order volumes, and fulfillment performance across different geographic regions and customer segments.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Tools Used
* **Data Visualization & BI Tool:** Power BI Desktop (Report View, Data Pane, Format Visual Pane)
* **Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset (`train.csv`)
* **Core Components & Visuals:**
  * **KPI Summary Cards:** Total Sales (`Sum of Sales`) and Total Orders (`Count of Postal Code`) for instant metric tracking.
  * **Column & Area Charts:** Detailed breakdown of sales performance across Product Categories and Sub-Categories.
  * **Donut Chart:** Regional sales distribution highlighting performance across West, East, Central, and South zones.
  * **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering capabilities based on Customer Segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office).
* **Design & Formatting:** Custom visual containers, optimized font scaling, clean axis title formatting, and professional layout alignment.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights & Features
* **Dynamic Interactivity:** Clicking on any segment or region dynamically filters all related visuals to drill down into specific subsets of data.
* **Product Performance Tracking:** Identifies top-performing product sub-categories to optimize inventory and marketing strategies.
* **Regional Distribution:** Visualizes market penetration across multiple geographic regions to identify high-growth areas.
